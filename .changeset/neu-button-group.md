Add `ButtonGroup` and `ButtonGroupText` (`@neomorphic-ui/react/button-group`): joins `Button`s into one raised slab split by grooves, like the kit's ‹ › stepper. Segments rest flat in the slab and sink into it while held or `aria-pressed`; disabled segments keep their place. `orientation` (`horizontal`, `vertical`) and `shape` (`rounded`, `pill`) set the axis and outer corners, which the end segments follow. `ButtonGroupText` adds a non-interactive segment such as a "2 / 5" readout. The group is a `role="group"`, so label it.
