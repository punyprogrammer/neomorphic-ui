Add `Textarea` (`@neomorphic-ui/react/textarea`) and `Select` (`@neomorphic-ui/react/select`).

- `Textarea`: the kit's message field, a well that deepens on focus. Resizes vertically, or grows with its text when `autoResize` (`field-sizing`). `aria-invalid` ring, shallower well when read-only.
- `Select`, built on Base UI: `SelectTrigger` is the kit's raised, uppercase "SELECT CATEGORY" with a solid caret, pressed in while open (sizes `sm`, `md`, `lg`). `SelectContent` is a raised card whose highlighted item sinks in; the chosen item carries a check. Plus `SelectValue` (with `placeholder`), `SelectLabel` (wired to the trigger), `SelectItem`, `SelectGroup`, `SelectGroupLabel` and a grooved `SelectSeparator`; long lists scroll.
