Add `Button` (`@neomorphic-react/ui/button`): raised at rest, pressed while held or `aria-pressed`, flat when disabled. Takes any icon element through `icon`, placed with `iconPosition` (`start`, `end`, `top`, `bottom`); with no children it becomes an icon tile (`shape="pill"` for a circle). Variants `default`, `primary`, `destructive`, `ghost`; sizes `sm`, `md`, `lg`; `isLoading` keeps focus and the raised look. `buttonVariants` styles links. Darkens `--destructive` to `oklch(0.52 0.2 27.325)` so red text passes AA on raised surfaces.
