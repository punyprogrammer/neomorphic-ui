Add `Chip` (`@neomorphic-react/ui/chip`): a status pill with an optional icon and a short label, such as "Paid" or "Due soon". `icon` takes any element and is decorative; `iconPosition` (`start`, `end`) follows the writing direction. Variants `default` (raised), `inset`, and token fills `primary`, `destructive`, `success`, `warning` paired with their `-foreground`; sizes `sm`, `md`, `lg`. Renders a `<span>`; `render` swaps the element (e.g. `<li>`). `Badge` stays for counts and dots.
